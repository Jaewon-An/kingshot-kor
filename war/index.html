Swap out lightly injured troops

Lightly injured troops can't fight again until their squad goes home. A squad that stays out gets weaker with every hit — so recall it and send a fresh one.

What the game says

“Lightly Injured Troops will recover when they are returned to the City. Until they are returned, the Lightly Injured Troops will stay in the same squad and not be able to participate in battles.”

We won, and still 79,915 of our troops are out of the fight. They are not dead and don't need the hospital — but any squad that stays out after a fight (holding the castle or a turret, or reinforcing it) keeps them, and they can't fight the next rally.

Why it matters

  1. Strength drops quietly. The squad is still there, but only its healthy troops fight the next rally. Every hit leaves the garrison weaker than it looks.
  2. Injured troops hold the space. They stay in the squad, using room that fresh troops could fill. Only a recall clears it.
  3. The fix is free. They recover the moment they reach the city — no healing time, no hospital. A swap only costs march time.
  4. The next rally decides it. We don't lose the castle to the rally that already hit — we lose it to the next one. Every enemy rally should meet a full-strength garrison.

How to swap

  1. After every fight, open your battle report and check Lightly Injured.
  2. If your squad has injured troops, recall it. They recover as soon as it reaches your city.
  3. Send a full squad back right away — the same one once it's home, or another one.
  4. Swap one squad at a time, right after an enemy rally lands. Don't all recall together — never leave the castle empty.
  5. Keep your city in your spot on the Castle Layout. Short marches make swaps fast.
